About us

Poachers turned gamekeepers.

Pat and John Horgan founded Midwestern Lift Services and ran it for nearly 30 years, growing it to over 200 staff, more than 5,000 lifts under service and over 700 new installations a year, operating in Ireland, the UK, Germany and Spain, and working with lift manufacturers from across Europe and Asia.

For three decades we sat on the other side of the table, selling lifts, writing service contracts and running the engineers. We know the clauses that quietly cut your cover and the shortcuts a busy contractor might take. We know the difference between a good engineer and a bad one, and between a lift that has been reset and one that has actually been fixed. We know when a job needs a senior engineer, when a part should be replaced rather than patched, and how to spot a lift that needs more than a service before it lets you down.

Now all of that works for you. Engineers across the industry still ring us for advice, and we trained many of the inspectors carrying out statutory inspections around the country today. We know the standard that's out there, and we hold ourselves above it.

Outside work, we're heavily involved in our local GAA club, Mungret St Paul's, and in helping build the community around it. We also love a round of golf, however badly we play it. If a deal ever needs to be done over 18 holes, count us in.
